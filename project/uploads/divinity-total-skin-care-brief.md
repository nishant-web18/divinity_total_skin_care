| Mera Jaipur | Tue & Thu only, 08:30–12:30 & 17:00–20:00; Sun 08:30–12:30 |

**Most likely reality:** a standard Jaipur clinic split shift — roughly **10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** and **5:00 PM – 8:00 PM**, Mon–Sat, with a shorter Sunday morning. But do not guess on a live medical site.

**[CONFIRM] Ask the client:** exact OPD hours per day, per doctor, and whether Sunday is open.

**Pitch angle:** "A patient searching at 7 PM sees four different timings and no website. Half of them call the clinic next door instead." This one slide sells the project.

## 9. Conversion essentials (non-negotiable for a clinic site)

- **Sticky mobile bar:** Call · WhatsApp · Directions. 80%+ of traffic will be mobile.
- **WhatsApp pre-filled message:** `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi,%20I%20want%20to%20book%20an%20appointment%20at%20Divinity%20Total%20Skin%20Care`
- **Appointment form:** name, phone, treatment interest, preferred slot — keep it to 4 fields max
- **Speed:** target LCP under 2.5s on 4G. Compress every image. This matters more than animation.
- **Google Maps embed** with the exact place ID above so directions open correctly
- **Hindi/English toggle or bilingual key sections** — Mansarovar patient base will appreciate it
- Medical disclaimer + "results vary" line on every treatment page
