White card for embedded product UI — always the surface under a chat mockup, chart or screenshot.

```jsx
<ProductCard title="Today" meta="3 new" hairline>
  <PhoneMockup messages={msgs} />
</ProductCard>
```

- White fill, radius 16px, 24px padding (or `flush` for full-bleed media), optional hairline.
- Keep the inner product surface white; the pastel belongs to the card around it.
