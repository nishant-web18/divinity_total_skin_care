Partner proof strip — a caption label above a single centred row of monochrome logos.

```jsx
<LogoStrip label="In partnership with" items={["Ministry of Health", "UNICEF", "Praekelt"]} />
```

- Label 12px/500 uppercase; logos ~24px tall with 48px gaps.
- Text stand-ins render in Mint Wash on dark bands (the reference’s #3d3d3d would fail contrast) and Graphite on light ones.
