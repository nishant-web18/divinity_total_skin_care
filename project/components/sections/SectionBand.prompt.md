Page-composition primitive: one band per "room". Alternate tones so the page reads as coloured rooms rather than continuous flow.

```jsx
<SectionBand tone="canvas">
  <h2 style={{ fontSize: "var(--text-heading-lg)" }}>Your entire patient journey</h2>
</SectionBand>
```

- 80px vertical padding, 80px gutters, 1280px inner max-width.
- Never put two dark bands next to each other; pastel or white always separates them.
