Every photograph in the system goes through this frame — large rounded crops of real clinic and community portraits.

```jsx
<PhotoFrame src="/assets/photos/clinician.jpg" alt="Community health worker" overlay="leaf" ratio="4 / 5" />
```

- Radius 24px by default, no shadow, `objectFit: cover`.
- `overlay="leaf" | "green" | "indigo"` applies the diagonal brand wash used in the toolkit band.
- Without `src` it shows a dashed placeholder — never substitute illustration or stock-looking art.
