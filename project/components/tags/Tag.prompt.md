Small pill label — use it to mark a category or state inside body content; tint it with the pastel of the card it sits on.

```jsx
<Tag tone="mint">Appointments</Tag>
<Tag outline="leaf">Live</Tag>
```

- 12px weight 600, padding 4px 12px, radius 9999px.
- `tone` fills with a pastel wash; `onDark` is the translucent variant for green bands.
- `outline` renders transparent with a Leaf Bright / Deep Teal / Indigo border instead.
